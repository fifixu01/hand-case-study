Abstract

Hands. Most people have two of them. But whose are the best? In this groundbreaking study, two researchers with zero qualifications examined the backs of seven hands belonging to Claytron, Gab, Allan, Jason, Furtoe, David and Eggsnut, and ranked them across several extremely important categories. Our findings will change nothing.

Methodology

  • Each subject was asked to photograph the back of their hand next to a quarter for scale.
  • Three subjects used a quarter. One used a ring. One used a fitness tracker. One used some cabinets. One sent a video game profile picture.
  • Each researcher ranked every hand on their own, by looking at it really hard. The two rankings were then combined using math.
  • No hands were touched in the making of this study.
